# RED SOL - submission drafts (Team RezonX Elites)

## Project summary
RED SOL is a free browser game for ages 7-12. You play one of three astronauts at a 3D Mars outpost while two AI crewmates work on their own. Dust storms, solar radiation surges, space rocks and broken machines arrive over 7 sols (Mars days). Every response is physical work in the base (fold panels, pull the shield lever, carry a repair kit) and every choice costs power, supplies, crew health or time. Radio delay to Earth makes waiting for advice a real trade-off.

## Project details
- 3 core meters: Power, Supplies (water and food), Crew Health (with a radiation dose).
- Trade-offs: shield level vs battery; folded panels vs no solar power; shelter vs lost work time; calling Earth vs the radio delay; limited repair kits.
- Events vary each run (seeded director); failure is an "Early Return" with a Lesson Learned perk, not a game over.
- Debrief links your choices to the real events they are based on, with ARCHIVED or GAME-MADE labels.
- Browser only, no login, offline after first load, quality settings (Auto/Low/Medium/High), desktop and phone landscape.

## 30-second demo script
0-4s title + crew select. 4-10s walk the base, AI crew working. 10-18s red warning "DUST STORM IN 0:45", fold panels. 18-25s radiation surge, shield dome ripples, shelter. 25-30s debrief with ARCHIVED data cards + "RezonX Elites".
